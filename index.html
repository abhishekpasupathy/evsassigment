EXPECTED IMPACT

Why earlier detection matters.

Expected benefits of a properly trained, deployed and maintained system. These are intended outcomes, not measured results from this prototype.

01

Earlier investigation

Combines image and community observations to help flag possible incidents sooner.

02

Reduced response delay

Structured location and evidence can help responders assess and prioritise reports.

03

Forest ecosystem protection

Timely intervention may reduce damage to trees, wildlife habitats and biodiversity.

04

Lower pollution impact

Preventing fire spread can limit smoke, particulate pollution and greenhouse-gas emissions.

05

Better environmental records

Consistent incident logs can support hotspot analysis and prevention planning.

✳

Technology with a purpose.

Human verification stays in the loop. A model score is a signal to investigate, never proof of a fire.
